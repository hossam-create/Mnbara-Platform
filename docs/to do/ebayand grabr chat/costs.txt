The time and cost to complete a project of this scale and complexity can vary widely based on several factors including project scope, team size, geographic location of the team, and the specific technologies used. Here is an estimation breakdown, keeping in mind these are very rough estimates and can vary significantly in practice.

Time to Complete the Project
Initial Planning and Design (Phase 1 & 2): 4-6 Weeks
Development Sprints (Phase 3): Given iterative 2-week sprints, for a comprehensive platform, you might be looking at approximately 4-6 months. Complex features or revisions could extend this.
Testing & Pre-Launch (Phase 4 & 5): 2-3 Months
Launch and Post-Launch Adjustments (Phase 6): 1-2 Months initially, but ongoing development and maintenance will be necessary.
Total Estimated Time: 7-12 Months for an MVP with continuous iterations post-launch.

Proposed Cost
The cost is primarily driven by team salaries, which vary significantly by location. Here’s a simplified breakdown assuming a mix of mid to senior-level professionals:

Developers: $70,000 - $150,000/year per developer, varying greatly by country. A team of around 7-10 developers could therefore cost between $490,000 to $1,500,000 annually.
UX/UI Designer: $60,000 - $120,000/year.
Project Manager/Product Owner: $80,000 - $150,000/year.
QA Engineers: $50,000 - $100,000/year per QA engineer.
DevOps Engineer: $80,000 - $150,000/year.
Additional Costs (Hosting, Tools, and Licenses): Can range from $10,000 - $50,000/year depending on the scale, tools, and cloud services used.
Total Estimated Cost for 1 Year: Assuming a team in the mid-salary range and located in regions with intermediate costs, the annual cost could be around $1,000,000 to $2,000,000, including salaries and additional costs.

Suggested Countries for Technical Success
United States & Canada: High cost but access to a large pool of talent and cutting-edge technologies. Particularly strong in innovation hubs like Silicon Valley, New York, Toronto, and Vancouver.

Eastern Europe (Poland, Ukraine, Romania): Offers a good balance between cost and quality. Renowned for technical education and a growing IT sector. Time zone differences can be advantageous for continuous work cycles.

India: Known for its vast IT talent pool and competitive costs. Excellent for software development outsourcing with established companies specializing in various technology stacks.

Latin America (Argentina, Brazil): Emerging tech hubs with growing IT sectors. Offers nearshore advantages for North American companies regarding time zones and cultural alignment.

Note on Remote Work
Given the rise of remote work, you can assemble a distributed team that combines talents from different regions to balance cost, skill, and project requirements effectively. Tools like Slack, Zoom, Jira, and GitHub support remote collaboration, making it possible to manage a global team.

Conclusion
Realistically, the complexity of your project means continuous investment in development, maintenance, and marketing beyond the initial launch. The estimated costs and timeframes provide a starting point, but you should be prepared for variability and remain adaptive to the project’s evolving needs. Engaging with local tech communities, exploring government incentives for startups, and networking within tech incubators or accelerators can also provide additional support and resources
