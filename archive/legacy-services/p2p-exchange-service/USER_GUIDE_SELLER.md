## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before you can sell currency, you need:

1. **Verified Account**: Complete email and phone verification
2. **Security Deposit**: Maintain at least 5% of your transaction value
3. **Trust Level**: Start with BRONZE level (up to $1,000 per transaction)

### Setting Up Your Security Deposit

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security Deposit**
2. Click **Add Funds**
3. Enter amount (minimum $50 recommended)
4. Choose payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or bank transfer)
5. Complete payment

**Why Security Deposit?**
- Protects buyers from fraud
- Shows your commitment
- Unlocks higher transaction limits
- Refundable when you stop trading

## Creating a Sell Request

### Step 1: Navigate to Exchange

1. Click **Exchange** in the main menu
2. Click **Create Request**
3. Select **SELL**

### Step 2: Enter Details

**Currency Pair**:
- **I have**: Select the currency you want to sell (e.g., EGP)
- **I want**: Select the currency you want to receive (e.g., USD)

**Amount**:
- Enter the amount you want to sell
- The system will calculate the equivalent amount based on current rates

**Exchange Rate**:
- **Market Rate**: Use the current market rate (recommended)
- **Custom Rate**: Set your own rate (may take longer to match)

**Expiration**:
- Choose how long your request stays active (1 hour to 7 days)
- Longer expiration = higher chance of matching

### Step 3: Review Fees

The system will show:
- **Platform Fee**: 1% of transaction value
- **Protection Fee**: 0.5% for buyer protection
- **Total Fees**: Combined fees
- **You Will Receive**: Final amount after fees

### Step 4: Confirm and Submit

1. Review all details
2. Check that you have sufficient security deposit
3. Click **Create Sell Request**
4. Your request is now live in the marketplace!

## Understanding Matches

### Automatic Matching

The system automatically matches your request with compatible buy requests every 30 seconds.

**Match Criteria**:
- Opposite currency pair (you sell EGP→USD, buyer wants USD→EGP)
- Similar amounts (within 10%)
- Compatible rates (within 5%)
- Both users have sufficient security deposit
- Both users meet trust level requirements

### Manual Matching

Buyers can also manually accept your request from the marketplace.

### Match Notification

When matched, you'll receive:
- Email notification
- In-app notification
- SMS notification (if enabled)

### Match Details

Click on the match to see:
- Buyer information (username, trust level, success rate)
- Transaction details (amounts, rate, fees)
- Settlement method (internal or external escrow)
- Countdown timers for each step

## Receiving Payment

### Step 1: Wait for Payment Initiation

The buyer has **30 minutes** to initiate payment.

**What happens**:
- Buyer selects payment method
- Buyer provides payment details
- You receive notification

### Step 2: Provide Payment Details

When the buyer initiates payment, provide your payment details:

**Bank Transfer**:
- Bank name
- Account number
- Account holder name
- SWIFT/IBAN (for international)

**Mobile Money**:
- Provider name
- Phone number
- Account name

**Crypto Wallet**:
- Wallet address
- Network (BTC, ETH, etc.)

### Step 3: Wait for Payment

The buyer has **60 minutes** to send payment and upload proof.

**During this time**:
- Monitor your payment account
- Check for incoming transfers
- Use the in-app chat if needed

**Important**: 
- Do NOT share your payment details outside the platform
- Do NOT accept payment through other channels
- All communication must happen in-app

## Confirming Receipt

### Step 1: Verify Payment

When you receive payment:

1. Check your bank/wallet account
2. Verify the amount matches the transaction
3. Verify the sender matches the buyer's details
4. Check the payment reference (if provided)

### Step 2: Review Proof of Payment

The buyer will upload proof of payment (screenshot or receipt).

**Check the proof**:
- Is it clear and readable?
- Does it show the correct amount?
- Does it show the correct recipient?
- Does it show the transaction date/time?

### Step 3: Confirm Receipt

If everything is correct:

1. Click **Confirm Receipt**
2. Review the confirmation details
3. Click **Yes, I Received Payment**

**What happens next**:
- Your currency is transferred to the buyer
- Buyer's currency is transferred to you
- Security deposits are unfrozen
- Match is marked as completed
- Your trust level is updated

### Step 4: Rate the Buyer

After completion, you can rate the buyer:
- 5 stars: Excellent experience
- 4 stars: Good experience
- 3 stars: Average experience
- 2 stars: Poor experience
- 1 star: Very poor experience

**Optional**: Leave a comment about your experience

## Security Tips

### ✅ DO

1. **Verify Payment**: Always check your account before confirming
2. **Use In-App Chat**: All communication must be in-platform
3. **Check Proof**: Review proof of payment carefully
4. **Report Issues**: Report suspicious activity immediately
5. **Keep Records**: Save transaction receipts
6. **Update Details**: Keep your payment details current
7. **Monitor Timeouts**: Watch the countdown timers
8. **Build Trust**: Complete exchanges successfully to upgrade trust level

### ❌ DON'T

1. **Share Contact Info**: Never share phone, email, or social media
2. **Accept External Payment**: Never accept payment outside the platform
3. **Rush Confirmation**: Take time to verify payment
4. **Ignore Red Flags**: Report suspicious behavior
5. **Share Account Details**: Never share login credentials
6. **Accept Partial Payment**: Only confirm full payment
7. **Skip Verification**: Always verify before confirming
8. **Engage in Disputes**: Use the dispute system if issues arise

### 🚨 Red Flags

Report immediately if buyer:
- Asks to communicate outside the platform
- Sends payment from a different account
- Sends partial payment
- Pressures you to confirm quickly
- Provides fake proof of payment
- Asks for refund outside the system
- Threatens or harasses you

## Troubleshooting

### Buyer Didn't Initiate Payment

**Timeout**: 30 minutes

**What happens**:
- Match is automatically cancelled
- Your request returns to marketplace
- Security deposits are unfrozen
- No penalty for you

**What to do**:
- Wait for automatic cancellation
- Or manually cancel after 30 minutes
- Create a new request if needed

### Buyer Didn't Send Payment

**Timeout**: 60 minutes after initiation

**What happens**:
- Dispute is automatically created
- Admin reviews the case
- You may need to provide evidence
- Decision made within 24-48 hours

**What to do**:
- Wait for automatic dispute
- Provide any relevant evidence
- Check your payment account thoroughly
- Respond to admin requests promptly

### Payment Amount is Wrong

**If amount is less than agreed**:
1. Do NOT confirm receipt
2. Contact buyer via in-app chat
3. Request correct amount
4. If buyer refuses, wait for timeout and dispute

**If amount is more than agreed**:
1. Contact buyer via in-app chat
2. Offer to refund the difference
3. Confirm receipt of correct amount
4. Process refund through the platform

### Proof of Payment is Unclear

**If you can't verify the proof**:
1. Request clearer proof via chat
2. Ask buyer to resend
3. Check your account anyway
4. If payment received, confirm
5. If not received, wait for timeout

### Buyer is Unresponsive

**If buyer doesn't respond**:
1. Send message via in-app chat
2. Wait for timeout periods
3. System will handle automatically
4. Dispute will be created if needed

### Technical Issues

**If you experience technical problems**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Contact support: support@mnbarh.com
5. Check status page: status.mnbarh.com

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does matching take?

- **Automatic**: Usually within 1 hour
- **Manual**: Instant when buyer accepts
- **Depends on**: Rate, amount, market demand

### Can I cancel after matching?

- **Before payment**: Yes, but may affect trust level
- **After payment**: No, must complete or dispute
- **Penalty**: Repeated cancellations lower trust level

### What if I don't receive payment?

- Wait for timeout (60 minutes)
- Dispute is created automatically
- Admin reviews within 24-48 hours
- Decision is final and binding

### How do I withdraw my funds?

- Go to **Wallet** → **Withdraw**
- Choose withdrawal method
- Enter amount
- Confirm withdrawal
- Funds arrive in 1-3 business days

### Can I sell multiple currencies?

- Yes, create separate requests for each
- Each request is independent
- Security deposit covers all active requests
- Trust level applies to all transactions
