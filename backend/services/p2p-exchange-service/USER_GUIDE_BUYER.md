## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before you can buy currency, you need:

1. **Verified Account**: Complete email and phone verification
2. **Security Deposit**: Maintain at least 5% of your transaction value
3. **Trust Level**: Start with BRONZE level (up to $1,000 per transaction)
4. **Payment Method**: Bank account, mobile money, or crypto wallet

### Setting Up Your Security Deposit

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security Deposit**
2. Click **Add Funds**
3. Enter amount (minimum $50 recommended)
4. Choose payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or bank transfer)
5. Complete payment

**Why Security Deposit?**
- Protects sellers from fraud
- Shows your commitment
- Unlocks higher transaction limits
- Refundable when you stop trading

## Creating a Buy Request

### Step 1: Navigate to Exchange

1. Click **Exchange** in the main menu
2. Click **Create Request**
3. Select **BUY**

### Step 2: Enter Details

**Currency Pair**:
- **I want**: Select the currency you want to buy (e.g., USD)
- **I have**: Select the currency you want to pay with (e.g., EGP)

**Amount**:
- Enter the amount you want to buy
- The system will calculate the equivalent amount based on current rates

**Exchange Rate**:
- **Market Rate**: Use the current market rate (recommended)
- **Custom Rate**: Set your own rate (may take longer to match)

**Expiration**:
- Choose how long your request stays active (1 hour to 7 days)
- Longer expiration = higher chance of matching

### Step 3: Review Fees

The system will show:
- **Platform Fee**: 1% of transaction value
- **Protection Fee**: 0.5% for buyer protection
- **Total Fees**: Combined fees
- **You Will Pay**: Total amount including fees

### Step 4: Confirm and Submit

1. Review all details
2. Check that you have sufficient security deposit
3. Click **Create Buy Request**
4. Your request is now live in the marketplace!

## Browsing the Marketplace

### Finding Sell Requests

1. Click **Exchange** → **Marketplace**
2. Browse available sell requests
3. Use filters to narrow down options

### Filters

**Currency Pair**:
- Select the currencies you want to exchange

**Amount Range**:
- Minimum and maximum amounts

**Exchange Rate**:
- Filter by rate range

**Seller Reputation**:
- Filter by trust level (BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD, PLATINUM)
- Higher levels = more reliable sellers

### Sorting

Sort results by:
- **Best Rate**: Most favorable exchange rate
- **Amount**: Highest or lowest amount
- **Reputation**: Highest trust level first
- **Newest**: Most recent requests

### Seller Information

Each listing shows:
- **Username**: Seller's display name
- **Trust Level**: BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD, or PLATINUM
- **Success Rate**: Percentage of successful exchanges
- **Completed Exchanges**: Total number of completed transactions
- **Average Completion Time**: How fast they complete exchanges
- **Rating**: Average rating from buyers

### Accepting a Request

1. Click on a sell request to view details
2. Review seller information
3. Check exchange rate and fees
4. Click **Accept Request**
5. Confirm acceptance

**What happens next**:
- Match is created
- Both security deposits are frozen
- You have 30 minutes to initiate payment

## Making Payment

### Step 1: Initiate Payment

After accepting a request:

1. Click **Initiate Payment**
2. Select your payment method
3. Provide your payment details (for seller to send you their details)
4. Click **Confirm**

**You have 30 minutes** to initiate payment, or the match will be cancelled.

### Step 2: Get Seller's Payment Details

The seller will provide their payment details:

**Bank Transfer**:
- Bank name
- Account number
- Account holder name
- SWIFT/IBAN (for international)

**Mobile Money**:
- Provider name
- Phone number
- Account name

**Crypto Wallet**:
- Wallet address
- Network (BTC, ETH, etc.)

### Step 3: Send Payment

**Important**:
- Send the EXACT amount shown
- Use the payment method you selected
- Include any reference number provided
- Keep proof of payment (screenshot or receipt)

**You have 60 minutes** to send payment and upload proof.

### Payment Methods

**Bank Transfer**:
1. Log into your online banking
2. Add seller as beneficiary
3. Enter exact amount
4. Add reference (if provided)
5. Confirm transfer
6. Take screenshot of confirmation

**Mobile Money**:
1. Open mobile money app
2. Select "Send Money"
3. Enter seller's phone number
4. Enter exact amount
5. Confirm transaction
6. Take screenshot of confirmation

**Crypto Wallet**:
1. Open your wallet app
2. Enter seller's wallet address
3. Select correct network
4. Enter exact amount
5. Confirm transaction
6. Save transaction hash

## Uploading Proof

### Step 1: Prepare Proof

**Acceptable Proof**:
- Bank transfer confirmation screenshot
- Mobile money receipt
- Crypto transaction hash/screenshot
- Payment receipt (PDF or image)

**Requirements**:
- Clear and readable
- Shows full transaction details
- Shows correct amount
- Shows correct recipient
- Shows date and time
- File size: Max 10MB
- Format: JPEG, PNG, or PDF

### Step 2: Upload Proof

1. Click **Upload Proof of Payment**
2. Select file from your device
3. Add notes (optional but recommended)
4. Click **Upload**

**Notes to include**:
- Payment method used
- Transaction reference number
- Any special circumstances
- Expected arrival time (if applicable)

### Step 3: Wait for Confirmation

The seller has **30 minutes** to confirm receipt.

**What happens**:
- Seller checks their account
- Seller reviews your proof
- Seller confirms receipt
- Funds are transferred
- Match is completed

### Verification Process

**Automatic Verification** (Coming Soon):
- AI checks proof validity
- Instant verification for clear proofs
- Reduces waiting time

**Manual Verification**:
- Seller reviews proof
- Seller checks their account
- Seller confirms receipt
- Or admin reviews if disputed

## Security Tips

### ✅ DO

1. **Verify Seller**: Check trust level and ratings
2. **Use In-App Chat**: All communication must be in-platform
3. **Send Exact Amount**: Match the transaction amount precisely
4. **Keep Proof**: Save all payment confirmations
5. **Upload Clear Proof**: Ensure proof is readable
6. **Monitor Timeouts**: Watch the countdown timers
7. **Report Issues**: Report suspicious activity immediately
8. **Build Trust**: Complete exchanges successfully to upgrade trust level

### ❌ DON'T

1. **Share Contact Info**: Never share phone, email, or social media
2. **Pay Outside Platform**: Never send payment through other channels
3. **Send Partial Payment**: Always send full amount
4. **Ignore Red Flags**: Report suspicious behavior
5. **Share Account Details**: Never share login credentials
6. **Rush Payment**: Take time to verify seller details
7. **Skip Proof Upload**: Always upload proof
8. **Engage in Disputes**: Use the dispute system if issues arise

### 🚨 Red Flags

Report immediately if seller:
- Asks to communicate outside the platform
- Provides suspicious payment details
- Asks for payment to different account
- Pressures you to pay quickly
- Refuses to provide payment details
- Asks for additional fees
- Threatens or harasses you

## Troubleshooting

### Seller Didn't Provide Payment Details

**Timeout**: 30 minutes after initiation

**What happens**:
- Match is automatically cancelled
- Your request returns to marketplace
- Security deposits are unfrozen
- No penalty for you

**What to do**:
- Wait for automatic cancellation
- Or manually cancel after 30 minutes
- Create a new request or accept another

### Payment Failed

**If your payment fails**:
1. Check your account balance
2. Verify payment details
3. Try again
4. Contact your bank/provider
5. If still failing, cancel match and try another

**What to do**:
- Cancel the match
- Report the issue to support
- Try with a different seller

### Seller Didn't Confirm Receipt

**Timeout**: 30 minutes after proof upload

**What happens**:
- Dispute is automatically created
- Admin reviews the case
- You may need to provide additional evidence
- Decision made within 24-48 hours

**What to do**:
- Wait for automatic dispute
- Provide any additional evidence
- Respond to admin requests promptly
- Be patient during review

### Proof Upload Failed

**If upload fails**:
1. Check file size (max 10MB)
2. Check file format (JPEG, PNG, PDF)
3. Try compressing the image
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact support if persistent

**Alternative**:
- Take a new screenshot
- Convert to different format
- Use image compression tool
- Try from mobile device

### Seller Claims Non-Receipt

**If seller says they didn't receive payment**:
1. Double-check your payment confirmation
2. Verify you sent to correct account
3. Check transaction status with your bank
4. Provide additional proof if available
5. Wait for admin review

**What to provide**:
- Original proof of payment
- Bank statement showing debit
- Transaction reference number
- Communication with your bank
- Any other relevant evidence

### Technical Issues

**If you experience technical problems**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Contact support: support@mnbarh.com
5. Check status page: status.mnbarh.com

## Settlement Methods

### Internal Settlement

**When both users have platform wallets**:
- Instant transfer
- No external fees
- Fastest method
- Most secure

**How it works**:
1. Your payment is verified
2. Funds are transferred internally
3. You receive currency instantly
4. No waiting for bank transfers

### External Escrow

**When using external providers (crypto)**:
- Blockchain-based escrow
- Supports BTC, ETH, USDT
- Decentralized security
- Slightly higher fees

**How it works**:
1. Escrow address is created
2. You send crypto to escrow
3. Seller confirms receipt
4. Escrow releases to seller
5. You receive your currency

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does matching take?

- **Automatic**: Usually within 1 hour
- **Manual**: Instant when you accept
- **Depends on**: Rate, amount, market supply

### Can I cancel after matching?

- **Before payment**: Yes, but may affect trust level
- **After payment**: No, must complete or dispute
- **Penalty**: Repeated cancellations lower trust level

### What if seller doesn't confirm?

- Wait for timeout (30 minutes)
- Dispute is created automatically
- Admin reviews within 24-48 hours
- Decision is final and binding

### How do I receive my currency?

- **Internal**: Instantly to your platform wallet
- **External**: To your provided wallet/account
- **Withdrawal**: Available immediately after completion

### Can I buy multiple currencies?

- Yes, create separate requests for each
- Each request is independent
- Security deposit covers all active requests
- Trust level applies to all transactions
